## Preferred academic style

These apply to any academic text, opinion, or procedural filing:

- Sentences of varying length. Alternate short, sharp periods with longer argumentative constructions.
- Unexpected openings. Start paragraphs with concrete data, rhetorical questions, or impact lines. Never with generic formulas.
- Present authorial voice. First person plural with conviction (we sustain, it seems to us, we propose), without academic timidity.
- Concrete before abstract. Present the example, the datum, or the real case before the theoretical category.
- Diversified connectives. Avoid repeating "furthermore", "in this sense", "it is worth noting". Prefer organic transitions that follow from the argument itself.
- Reading rhythm. Build argumentative tension, do not merely list information. Each paragraph leads to the next.

## Anti-AI patterns (forbidden)

Do not open with: "It is important to highlight that...", "In the context of...", "In light of the foregoing...", "It is worth noting that...", "It is fundamental to understand that...". Avoid predictable and repetitive structures. Eliminate decorative emojis, excessive bullets, and typical chatbot formulas. Never use an em dash as a sentence connector.
